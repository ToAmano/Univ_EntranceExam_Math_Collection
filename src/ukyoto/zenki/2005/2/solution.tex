{\bf [解]}
各辺正から常用対数をとって，$\log_{10}x=\log x$とおくと，
\begin{align}
10\log2<n\log\dfrac{5}{4}<20\log2
\end{align}
\begin{align}
10\log2<n\cdot\log\dfrac{10}{8}<20\log2
\end{align}
\begin{align}
10\log2<n(1-3\log2)<20\log2
\end{align}

$\alpha=\log2$とおく．$1-3\alpha>0$から，
\begin{align}
\dfrac{10\alpha}{1-3\alpha}<n<\dfrac{20\alpha}{1-3\alpha}
\end{align}
\begin{align}
-\dfrac{10}{3}+\dfrac{10}{3(1-3\alpha)}<n<\dfrac{20}{3}\left(-1+\dfrac{1}{1-3\alpha}\right)\quad\cdots①
\end{align}

ここで，$0.301<\alpha<0.3011$から，
\begin{align}
0.0967<1-3\alpha<0.097
\end{align}
\begin{align}
\dfrac{1}{0.097}<\dfrac{1}{1-3\alpha}<\dfrac{1}{0.0967}
\end{align}
\begin{align}
10.30\ldots<\dfrac{1}{1-3\alpha}<10.34\ldots
\end{align}
\begin{align}
9.30\ldots<-1+\dfrac{1}{1-3\alpha}<9.34\ldots
\end{align}

となるので，
\begin{align}
31.0\ldots<\dfrac{10}{3}\left(-1+\dfrac{1}{1-3\alpha}\right)<31.13\ldots
\end{align}
\begin{align}
62.0\ldots<\dfrac{20}{3}\left(-1+\dfrac{1}{1-3\alpha}\right)<62.26\ldots
\end{align}

だから，①をみたす$n$は，$n=32,\ldots,62$の$62-32+1=31$個．
