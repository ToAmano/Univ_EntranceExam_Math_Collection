{\bf [解]}
\paragraph{(1)}

$f(x) = \dfrac{x+t}{x(1-tx)}$，
\begin{align}
f'(x) = \dfrac{t(x^2 + 2tx - 1)}{x^2 (1-tx)^2}
\end{align}
より，下表を得る:

\begin{table}[htb]
\centering
\caption{$f(x)$ の増減表}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c|c|c}
$x$ & $0$ & $\dots$ & $-t + \sqrt{t^2+1}$ & $\dots$ & $1$ \\ \hline
$f'$ & & $-$ & $0$ & $+$ & \\ \hline
$f$ & & $\searrow$ & & $\nearrow$ &
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

よって，$\alpha = -t + \sqrt{t^2+1}$ だから $\alpha' = \dfrac{-1}{\sqrt{t^2+1}} \alpha$．
\begin{align}
\dfrac{d}{dt} f(\alpha) = \dfrac{(\alpha' + 1) \alpha (1 - t\alpha) - (\alpha + t)(\alpha' - \alpha^2 - 2t\alpha \alpha')}{\alpha^2 (1 - t\alpha)^2}
\end{align}
この分子 $g(\alpha)$ として
\begin{align}
g(\alpha) &= \alpha (\alpha' + 1 - t\alpha\alpha' - t\alpha) - (\alpha + t)(\alpha' - \alpha^2 - 2t\alpha\alpha') \\
&= \alpha (\alpha^2 - t\alpha + 1 + t\alpha\alpha') - t\alpha' + t\alpha^2 + 2t^2 \alpha\alpha' \\
&= \alpha^3 + t\alpha^2\alpha' + \alpha - t\alpha' + 2t^2 \alpha\alpha' \\
&= \dfrac{\alpha}{\sqrt{t^2+1}} \Bigl[ \alpha (1 - 2t\sqrt{t^2+1}) + t + \sqrt{t^2+1} \Bigr] \\
&= 2\alpha \Bigl( t^2 - t\sqrt{t^2+1} + 1 \Bigr)
\end{align}
だから下表を得る:

\begin{table}[htb]
\centering
\caption{$\alpha, f(\alpha)$ の $t$ に関する増減表}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c}
$t$ & $0$ & $\dots$ & $1$ \\ \hline
$d\alpha/dt$ & & $-$ & \\ \hline
$df/dt$ & & $+$ & \\ \hline
$(\alpha, f(\alpha))$ & $(1, 1)$ & $\nwarrow$ & $(\sqrt{2}-1, 3-2\sqrt{2})$
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\paragraph{(2)}

$-t + \sqrt{t^2+1} \le t \iff \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \le t \quad (\because 0 < t < 1 \text{ だから (1) より})$
\begin{align}
\beta = \begin{cases} -t + \sqrt{t^2+1} & \Bigl( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \le t \le 1 \Bigr) \quad \cdots \text{①} \\ t & \Bigl( 0 < t \le \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \Bigr) \quad \cdots \text{②} \end{cases}
\end{align}
であり，②の時
\begin{align}
f(\beta) &= \dfrac{2\beta}{\beta (1-\beta^2)} = \dfrac{2}{1-\beta^2} \\
&\quad \Bigl( 0 < \beta \le \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \Bigr)
\end{align}
は単調増加関数である．これと (1) より，グラフは下図:
