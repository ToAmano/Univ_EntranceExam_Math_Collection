{\bf[解]} $f(-0.2)=A$，$f(-0.1)=B$，$f(0)=C$，$f(0.1)=D$，$f(0.2)=E$とおく．さらに
\begin{align}
 f(x)=ax^4+bx^3+cx^2+dx+e 
\end{align}
とする．実際に計算して
\begin{align}
C&=e \\
A&=\dfrac{a}{625}-\dfrac{b}{125}+\dfrac{c}{25}-\dfrac{d}{5}+e \label{eq:1}\\
E&=\dfrac{a}{625}+\dfrac{b}{125}+\dfrac{c}{25}+\dfrac{d}{5}+e \label{eq:2}\\
B&=\dfrac{a}{10000}-\dfrac{b}{1000}+\dfrac{c}{100}-\dfrac{d}{10}+e\label{eq:3} \\
E&=\dfrac{a}{10000}+\dfrac{b}{1000}+\dfrac{c}{100}+\dfrac{d}{10}+e\label{eq:4} 
\end{align}
$\text{\cref{eq:1}}-\text{\cref{eq:2}}$，$\text{\cref{eq:3}}-\text{\cref{eq:4}}$から
\begin{align}
E-A&=\dfrac{2b}{125}+\dfrac{2d}{5} \\
D-B&=\dfrac{2b}{1000}+\dfrac{2d}{10}
\end{align}
ここから$b$を消す．題意の値に注意して
\begin{align}
\dfrac{6d}{5}&=A-8B+8D-E \\
&=3.258 \\
\therefore d&=2.715
\end{align}
故に，$f'(0)=d=2.715\cdots$(答)　となる．
