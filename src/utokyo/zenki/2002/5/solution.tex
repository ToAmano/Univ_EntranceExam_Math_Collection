{\bf [解]}
$Q_k(0,0,q_k)$とおく．$\triangle OP_kQ_k$に三平方の定理を用いて，
\begin{align}
1=q_k^2+\left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^2+\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^2\quad\therefore\ q_k=\sqrt{2\cdot\dfrac{k}{n}-2\left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^2}\quad(>0)
\end{align}

さらに，$\triangle OP_kP_{k+1}$の面積$S_k$は，サラスの公式により
\begin{align}
S_k=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\dfrac{k}{n}\left(1-\dfrac{k+1}{n}\right)-\dfrac{k+1}{n}\left(1-\dfrac{k}{n}\right)\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{n}
\end{align}

だから，
\begin{align}
V_k=\dfrac{1}{3}S_kq_k=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6}\cdot\dfrac{1}{n}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{n}-\left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^2}
\end{align}

より，
\begin{align}
\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}V_k&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6}\int_0^1\sqrt{x-x^2}\,dx\\
&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6}\int_0^1\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{4}-\left(x-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2}\,dx\\
&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{48}\pi
\end{align}
