{\bf [解]}
\textbf{(1)}
\begin{align}
\dfrac{\sin(2n+1)x}{\sin x}=\dfrac{x}{\sin x}\cdot\dfrac{\sin(2n+1)x}{(2n+1)x}\cdot(2n+1)\xrightarrow{x\to0}2n+1
\end{align}

\textbf{(2)} $\displaystyle\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin(2n+1)x}{\sin x}dx=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\ \cdots$①を帰納法で示す．$n=1$の時，
\begin{align}
\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin3x}{\sin x}dx=\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}(3-4\sin^2x)dx=\left[3x\right]_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}-4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\left[x-\dfrac{1}{2}\sin2x\right]_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}
\end{align}
\begin{align}
=\dfrac{3}{2}\pi-2\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\pi
\end{align}
で成立．以下$n=k\in\mathbb{N}$での成立を仮定し，$n=k+1$での成立を示す．
\begin{align}
\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin(2k+3)x}{\sin x}dx=\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin(2k+1)x\cos2x+\sin2x\cos(2k+1)x}{\sin x}dx
\end{align}
\begin{align}
=\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin(2k+1)x}{\sin x}(1-2\sin^2x)dx+\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}2\cos x\cos(2k+1)x\,dx \quad\cdots\text{②}
\end{align}
であり，
\begin{align}
\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin(2k+1)x}{\sin x}dx=\dfrac{\pi}{2} \quad(\because\text{仮定})
\end{align}
\begin{align}
\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\sin x\sin(2k+1)x\,dx=0 \quad(\because k\ge1)
\end{align}
\begin{align}
\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\cos x\cos(2k+1)x\,dx=0 \quad(\because k\ge1)
\end{align}
だから，②に代入して
\begin{align}
\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin(2k+3)x}{\sin x}dx=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-0+0=\dfrac{\pi}{2}
\end{align}
より，$n=k+1$でも①は成立し，よって示された．
