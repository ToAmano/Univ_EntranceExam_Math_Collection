{\bf [解]}
$0<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}\ \cdots$①
\begin{align}
\begin{split}
f(x)&=\int_0^x\dfrac{d\theta}{\cos\theta}+\int_x^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{d\theta}{\sin\theta} \\
f'(x)&=\dfrac{1}{\cos x}-\dfrac{1}{\sin x}=\dfrac{S-C}{CS} \quad(S=\sin x,\ C=\cos x\text{から，下表を得る})
\end{split}
\end{align}
\begin{table}[htb]
  \centering
\begin{tabular}{c|ccccc}
$x$ & $0$ & & $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ & & $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ \\
\hline
$f'$ & & $-$ & $0$ & $+$ & \\
\hline
$f$ & & $\searrow$ & & $\nearrow$ &
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
したがって，$f(x)$は$x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$で最小．
\begin{align}
\begin{split}
\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{d\theta}{\sin\theta}&=\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^0\dfrac{-dt}{\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-t\right)} \quad\left(t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right) \\
&=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\dfrac{d\theta}{\cos\theta}
\end{split}
\end{align}
からこの時
\begin{align}
\begin{split}
f\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)&=2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\dfrac{d\theta}{\cos\theta} \\
&=2\int_0^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}\dfrac{1}{1-x^2}dx \\
&=\left[\log(x+1)-\log(1-x)\right]_0^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \\
&=\log\dfrac{1+\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{1-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}=\log\dfrac{2+\sqrt{2}}{2-\sqrt{2}}
\end{split}
\end{align}
