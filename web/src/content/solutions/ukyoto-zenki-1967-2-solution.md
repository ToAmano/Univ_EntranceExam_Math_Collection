[**解**] $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$

1.  $z^2 = x^2 - y^2 + 2xyi = i$ だから，複素数の相等から
    

$$
\begin{align*}
\begin{cases}
    x^2 - y^2 = 0 \\
    2xy = 1
    \end{cases}
    \iff (x, y) = \left( \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \right) \quad (\text{複号同順}) \quad \dots \text{①}
\end{align*}
$$

2.  $z = 2i \pm \sqrt{(2i)^2 - (-4+2i)}$
    

$$
\begin{align*}
\begin{aligned}
    &= 2i \pm \sqrt{-4 + 4 - 2i} \\
    &= 2i \pm \sqrt{-2i} \\
    &= 2i \pm \sqrt{2}i \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}i \right) \\
    &= -1 + 3i = x + yi \quad \dots \text{②}
    \end{aligned}
\end{align*}
$$

    だから，① 又は ② をみたせば良く
    

$$
\begin{align*}
(x, y) = \left( \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \right), (-1, 3)
\end{align*}
$$
